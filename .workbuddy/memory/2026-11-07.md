## 15:00 — SVG chart claim corrected: dependency, not capability
Owner challenged `REVIEW_ROUND5.md` §10.3 (*"A candlestick chart drawn with

that does not exist — the JS page needs a comment at `:389` to avoid `0.0`),

browser. Verdict: parity on the **ruled behaviour** plausible; parity as a
general charting library not claimed.
