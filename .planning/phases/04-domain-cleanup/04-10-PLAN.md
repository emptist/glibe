## The one implementation, and what it is NOT

`desk.gleam` already has one `get_*` per level. The missing executable fact is
that **the whole chain has no gap**: one pure read walks the three holders'
keys from `Desk` to a `Timeframe`.

```gleam
pub fn timeframe_under(
  desk: Desk,
  broker_id: BrokerId,
  symbol: Symbol,
  interval: Interval,
) -> Result(Timeframe, LookupError)
```

- `under` = follow the holder's key down one level at a time. All four
  arguments already exist in the module; **no address type, no new type, no
  new field, no new dependency**.
- It exists because `test/level_judgement_test.gleam` needs to pin R36 / R37 —
  **the test is where the design goal lives** — and its body is what step 4
  fills. No type or function is invented for the chain's sake. The chain is
  the whole making process of ONE module file (R19).
- It is a **read**. It is NOT `monitor.advance`, `TimeframeAddress`,
  `IncomingBar` or `MonitorError` — R33 ruled those out as non-owner
  imperative scheduling from a non-Gleam architecture. Nothing here
  advances, subscribes, schedules or keeps state.
- Its consumer is the test that pins R36/R37, and any later view or input
  boundary that must locate a timeframe by the keys its holders own.
- Errors stay the existing closed `LookupError` set; each variant carries the
  key that was asked for. No `Error(String)`, no catch-all arm.
