<objective>
Give every Instrument and every per-Interval Timeframe a typed owner.

Purpose: `src/glibe/config.gleam` holds two parallel lists keyed by composite
strings such as `"BTCUSDT_H1"`: `symbols: List(#(String, symbol.Symbol))` and
`timeframe_settings: List(#(String, indicator_settings.TimeframeSettings))`.
One `String` carries two facts (which instrument, which interval), so the
compiler cannot tell them apart and neither list has an owner. Ruling R20 says
the chain `Desk -> Broker -> Instrument -> Interval` is must-have. This plan
lands its first module as a documentation-first / TDD shell; it does NOT
migrate `config.gleam`.

Output: `src/glibe/desk.gleam`, `test/desk_test.gleam`, this plan's SUMMARY.
</objective>
