<rulings_fixed_by_owner>
- R28: the provider keeps its own native shape and its own standardisation. `/api/v3/klines` returns an ARRAY OF ARRAYS — its own wire shape — decoded in `binance/rest.gleam`, not folded into the 12-field record `binance/kline.gleam` (which is Binance's other, WS-facing shape).
- R33: history and live are ONE stream; the same `feed_sourcebar` entry carries both. No second path.
- R27: the wire's epoch milliseconds become one absolute `Timestamp` at this boundary and nowhere else.
- R23/R37: `Interval` has one home — the key of `Instrument.timeframes`. A REST fetch is told the interval by its caller (glib `binance.gleam:168` precedent) and maps it to the standard variant here; the interval is not smuggled in as a bar field.
- R13: errors are a domain type with one constructor per real failure, never `Error(String)`.
</rulings_fixed_by_owner>

<measured>
- `https://data-api.binance.vision/api/v3/klines?symbol=BTCUSDT&interval=1m&limit=1` returns HTTP 200 from this host, on the direct connection (no proxy) and through the proxy; body timestamp equals the current wall clock. `api.binance.com` and `api-gcp.binance.com` time out here — data-api.binance.vision is the reachable official public market-data host.
- Gleam `httpc.send(request.to(...))` reaches it and returns status 200 with the live body (probe 2026-11-25, removed).
- `gleam_json.parse` + `decode.field(0..7, ...)` decodes the kline arrays on the Erlang target. Indices 8+ are beyond the first eight list elements `decode.field` indexes; only fields 0–7 (open_time, open, high, low, close, volume, close_time, quote_asset_volume) are read — exactly the six a standard bar needs plus close_time.
</measured>
